Career FAQ

Common questions before starting a digital career.

Do I need to be great at math to become a Front-End Developer?

Not necessarily. To start front-end development, the most important things are understanding HTML structure, CSS styling, basic JavaScript logic, and consistently building practice projects.

What kind of portfolio is good for beginners?

A beginner portfolio should include 2 to 3 polished, responsive websites with simple interactions such as forms, filters, or menus.

How long until I can apply for jobs?

It depends on your learning intensity. With regular practice, you can start building a portfolio in a few weeks and keep improving it as you learn.

What skills should I learn after HTML, CSS, and JavaScript?

Once your foundation is strong, continue with responsive design, accessibility, Git, basic APIs, and front-end frameworks such as React.
